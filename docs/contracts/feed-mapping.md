# Feed mapping — CMS Synthetic Medicare to platform feeds

- **Ticket:** PAYER-11
- **Source:** CMS Synthetic Medicare Enrollment, FFS Claims and Part D Event (RIF format, May 2023 release)
- **Column layouts:** `docs/contracts/source_layouts/` (generated by `scripts/extract_layouts.py`)
- **Status:** Draft — to be confirmed by profiling in Snowflake (Sprint 2)

## Source facts
- All files are **pipe-delimited (`|`)** with a header row, despite the `.csv` extension.
- All 11 beneficiary yearly files have an **identical 185-column layout** (verified 2015–2025).
- Files contain **final-action claims only**: no original/adjustment/void history.

## Feed summary
| Feed | Source file | Columns | Grain | Business key | Driving date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| claims_professional | ffs_claims/carrier.csv | 96 | claim line | CLM_ID + LINE_NUM | NCH_WKLY_PROC_DT |
| claims_inpatient | ffs_claims/inpatient.csv | 197 | claim revenue-center line | CLM_ID + CLM_LINE_NUM | NCH_WKLY_PROC_DT |
| claims_outpatient | ffs_claims/outpatient.csv | 162 | claim revenue-center line | CLM_ID + CLM_LINE_NUM | NCH_WKLY_PROC_DT |
| pharmacy | pde/pde.csv | 36 | prescription fill | PDE_ID | PD_DT (fallback SRVC_DT) |
| eligibility | beneficiary/beneficiary_YYYY.csv | 185 | member per year | BENE_ID + BENE_ENROLLMT_REF_YR | BENE_ENROLLMT_REF_YR |

**Driving date principle:** claim feeds are split into daily deliveries by **processing/paid date**, not service date, because payer extracts send claims when they are adjudicated. Late-arriving claims (service weeks before delivery) therefore occur naturally.

## Feed details

### claims_professional (carrier)
- **Header-level columns** (repeated on each line): CLM_FROM_DT, CLM_THRU_DT, PRNCPAL_DGNS_CD, ICD_DGNS_CD1–12, CLM_PMT_AMT.
- **Line-level columns:** LINE_NUM, HCPCS_CD, LINE_NCH_PMT_AMT, PRF_PHYSN_NPI.
- **Providers:** PRF_PHYSN_NPI (performing, line level — used for provider reporting), ORG_NPI_NUM (billing organization), RFR_PHYSN_NPI (referring).
- **Member:** BENE_ID.
- **Modeling note:** split into claim header and claim line models in staging. Never sum CLM_PMT_AMT across lines (double counts); use line amounts or one row per claim.

### claims_inpatient
- **Stay:** CLM_ADMSN_DT, NCH_BENE_DSCHRG_DT; **payment driver:** CLM_DRG_CD.
- **Clinical:** ICD_DGNS_CD1…, ICD_PRCDR_CD1…; **revenue line:** REV_CNTR.
- **Providers:** PRVDR_NUM (facility CCN), ORG_NPI_NUM (facility NPI), AT_PHYSN_NPI (attending), OP_PHYSN_NPI (operating).
- **Modeling note:** same header/line split and double-count rule as professional.

### claims_outpatient
- **Revenue line:** REV_CNTR, HCPCS_CD, REV_CNTR_DT (line service date).
- **Providers:** PRVDR_NUM, ORG_NPI_NUM, AT_PHYSN_NPI. No admission/discharge/DRG.

### pharmacy (Part D events)
- **Drug:** PROD_SRVC_ID (NDC), QTY_DSPNSD_NUM, DAYS_SUPLY_NUM.
- **Amounts:** TOT_RX_CST_AMT, PTNT_PAY_AMT.
- **Providers:** PRSCRBR_ID (prescriber; NPI when PRSCRBR_ID_QLFYR_CD indicates NPI), SRVC_PRVDR_ID (pharmacy).
- **Note:** no diagnosis codes on pharmacy claims.

### eligibility (Master Beneficiary Summary)
- **Monthly repeating fields (_01–_12):** MDCR_ENTLMT_BUYIN_IND (Part A/B coverage), HMO_IND (Medicare Advantage), DUAL_STUS_CD (Medicaid dual status).
- **Demographics (PHI / protected — masking candidates):** BENE_BIRTH_DT, BENE_DEATH_DT, SEX_IDENT_CD, BENE_RACE_CD, STATE_CODE, ZIP_CD.
- **Modeling note:** unpivot to a **member-month** table (one row per member per covered month). Member months are the denominator for PMPM and utilization per 1,000.

## Joins
| From | Column | To |
|---|---|---|
| All claim feeds, pharmacy | BENE_ID | eligibility.BENE_ID (+ year/month of service) |
| Professional | PRF_PHYSN_NPI, ORG_NPI_NUM, RFR_PHYSN_NPI | provider dimension (NPI) |
| Inpatient / outpatient | ORG_NPI_NUM, AT_PHYSN_NPI, OP_PHYSN_NPI | provider dimension (NPI) |
| Pharmacy | PRSCRBR_ID (when NPI) | provider dimension (NPI) |

## Data quality rules identified
- Business keys must be unique per feed.
- Every claim BENE_ID should exist in eligibility for the service year.
- A member with HMO_IND showing Medicare Advantage in a month should have no FFS claims that month.
- NPIs must be 10 digits and pass the check digit.
- Service dates must not be after processing/paid dates.

## Known gaps: sample vs production
- **No claim versioning in source:** the simulator will generate originals, adjustments and voids.
- **No provider feed:** provider attributes will come from NPPES or be derived from NPIs on claims.
- **Eligibility is annual:** production-style weekly full + daily change files will be generated by the simulator.
